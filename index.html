Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Photo PDF Maker handles information when you use the app to select photos, scan documents, create and manage PDF files, and view advertisements.

App: Photo PDF Maker Developer: AlTricker Package: com.photo.pdf.maker Effective date: November 24, 2026 Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Photo PDF Maker is an Android application for importing images, scanning documents, creating and editing PDF files, and organizing PDF documents. The app is published by AlTricker ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy describes the information and device permissions associated with the reviewed Android release.

By using the app, you acknowledge the practices described here. Please do not use the app to process information that you are not authorized to access or share.

2. Information handled by the app

Photos, scans, and documents

When you choose images or scan pages, the app processes the selected image content to create, edit, and export PDF documents. Files and working data may include image content, PDF content, file names, page order, document properties, and temporary working copies. You choose which photos or documents to import.

App settings and document records

The app includes local document and project-draft storage and settings such as output preferences. These records may include document names, file locations, creation or modification details, and your selected app preferences.

Advertising-related information

The reviewed release includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Android advertising-ID permissions. When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may receive or process information such as the advertising ID (where available and permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad delivery and interaction events, and diagnostic information. The exact information and processing depend on your device, region, Google settings, and the ad configuration in the published version.

The reviewed app bundle contains Google's test AdMob application ID. Before publishing with live ads, the developer should verify the production ad configuration and ensure that this policy and the Google Play Data safety form accurately describe the live build.

3. How information is used

Information handled by the app and its advertising SDK may be used to:

  • Import images and scan pages that you select.
  • Create, edit, organize, preview, and export PDF documents.
  • Save local app settings, document records, and project drafts.
  • Display and measure advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and maintain the advertising service.
  • Maintain app functionality, troubleshoot errors, and protect the app and its users.

4. Device permissions and file access

  • Camera: Used for the document-scanning feature. The app requests camera access when you use the scanner. You can deny this permission; camera scanning will then be unavailable.
  • Photo selection: The app uses the Android Photo Picker to let you select images. The reviewed build's user-facing permission notice says photo selection uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker.
  • File import and export: The app uses Android's Storage Access Framework for user-selected file and folder operations. You choose the files or destination made available to the app.
  • Internet and network state: Internet access supports network features, including the advertising SDK; network-state access allows the app or included SDKs to check connectivity.
  • Advertising ID / Android ad services: The release declares advertising-related permissions used by the Google advertising stack.

The reviewed manifest does not declare permissions for location, contacts, or microphone access. Android, Google Play services, or third-party SDK components may have their own system-level behavior; consult their documentation for details.

5. Local storage, security, and retention

The reviewed release contains a local Room database for PDF document records and project drafts, along with PDF and image-processing components. Files that you create or export may be saved in an app-managed location or in a destination you select through Android. The app may also create temporary working files while processing documents.

Information stored on your device generally remains there until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior and any copies you have exported or shared. A file saved outside the app's private storage may remain after the app is uninstalled. You are responsible for managing exported copies and protecting access to your device.

We use the storage and platform protections provided by Android, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information. The reviewed release includes Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising and device information as described in Google's policies. The app also allows you to export or share documents using Android or other apps; when you choose to do so, the receiving app or service handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Analytics and diagnostics

No separate app-owned analytics or crash-reporting SDK was positively identified in the supplied release bundle. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information needed for advertising, diagnostics, fraud prevention, and service operation. If analytics, crash-reporting, or other measurement tools are added or enabled in a future release, this section must be updated to identify them and describe their data practices.

8. Children's privacy

Photo PDF Maker is not designed specifically for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service associated with the app, please contact us so that the issue can be reviewed. The developer must ensure that the app's audience, advertising configuration, and Google Play declarations comply with applicable children's privacy and families requirements.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

  • You can deny or later revoke camera permission in your Android device settings.
  • You control which images and documents you select through Android's file and photo selection interfaces.
  • You can delete documents from the app and remove exported copies using the relevant Android file-management tools.
  • You may be able to reset or limit your device's advertising ID and manage ad personalization through Android and Google settings. Available controls vary by device and region.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other handling of personal information. Contact the developer to make a request.

Google and other third-party service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing and safeguards are described in their own privacy documentation. The developer should add details about any additional cross-border processing if other remote services are used in production.
